# Brain Station 23 discovery notes

Research on 20 September 2026 found that Brain Station 23 publicly:

- describes itself as a global software company founded in 2006 with roughly 850–900+ technology professionals;
- lists staff augmentation, managed services, digital transformation, consulting and MVP development;
- markets pre-vetted talent and says engineers develop “from Trainees to Leads” with its in-house L&D team;
- supports remote, hybrid, flexi-remote and shared-talent engagements;
- groups talent across backend, frontend, mobile, CMS/LMS/ERP/CRM, cloud/infrastructure, data/analytics, QA, AI/ML and XR;
- offers cybersecurity, e-commerce, AEM, SharePoint, Moodle/LMS, game/3D, BI and enterprise software services;
- works across fintech, healthcare/pharma, telecom, retail, education, e-commerce, automotive, real estate, software/ITES, startups and non-profits.

## Product implications

Application permission, employment designation, level, job family, skills, organisational unit, availability and career target must be separate concepts. Because staff augmentation is a core offering, opportunity, interview, availability, allocation and evidence-backed readiness are plausible central workflows. That is an inference to validate with L&D; public pages do not expose internal processes or policy.

The SRS therefore uses a configurable role catalogue. Candidate titles such as Intern, Trainee, Software Engineer I/II, Senior Software Engineer I/II, Lead, AI/ML Engineer, QA Engineer, DevOps/Cloud Engineer, Data Engineer, Mobile Engineer, Designer, Business Analyst and Project Manager must be checked against official HR data. Scraping titles into code would fossilise today's naming mistakes.
